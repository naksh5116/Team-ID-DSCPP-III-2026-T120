VitalRoute

Open a role window

Each role opens in its own browser tab. Open as many as you like, on this computer or on other devices: they all work on the same hospital, and a change made in one appears in the others straight away.

Nurse Doctor Patient Super Admin
Nurse

Registers emergency and outpatient (OPD) patients, watches the waiting lists and runs the transfer desk.

Doctor

Treats the next patient, sees the walking route to the assigned bed, keeps patient records, books operation theatres.

Patient

Read-only: one patient's record, bill and walking directions. Has a full-screen wall display for a ward screen.

Super Admin

Everything, plus billing and discharge, admissions by date, staff, indexes, undo, save and reload.
